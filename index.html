Delivery, Payment & Returns

Where We Ship

We ship worldwide, with tracking on every order.

Delivery (jerseys & kits)

Made to order and shipped with tracking. Standard delivery takes about 3 – 4 weeks from design approval. Express (about 2 weeks) is available for an extra charge, so DM us for a quote. Free shipping on standard orders of more than 2 items.

Payment

You pay securely through a payment link we send you (powered by Monzo and Stripe). We send the link once you've confirmed your order, and production starts when payment is received.

Returns & Problems (jerseys & kits)

Every jersey is personalised, so we can’t accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or the wrong size. If your order is faulty, damaged or different from your approved design, message us within 5 days of delivery with photos and a video of you opening the parcel, and we’ll replace or refund it.
